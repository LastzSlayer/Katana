Season 2 Guide

Everfrost Era Prep

This guide helps Katana players prepare for Season 2/3 by focusing on seasonal buildings, resistance, NPC farming, Ice Pit, heroes, mastery, and weekly event priorities. Big thanks to Hades(Send him roses) from AOE for the PDF.

Quick Start Open PDF Guide
Priority Plan

First Week Focus

  • Kill Frostbanes daily for Frost Cores.
  • Level Greenhouse and BioLab quickly.
  • Build Virus Resistance to unlock stronger NPCs.
  • Save seasonal resources early for key buildings.
Quick start

First Week Checklist

🧊

Farm Daily

Kill your highest possible Frostbanes first. Use Snow Leopards after Frostbane attempts are done.

🏗️

Upgrade Buildings

Prioritize BioLab and Smart Greenhouse to increase resistance and Ice Bloom output.

🧬

Push Resistance

Higher Virus Resistance lets you defeat higher level seasonal NPCs for better rewards.

🎯

Time Your Kills

If your BioLab finishes soon, wait before using daily kills so you can farm higher levels.

Core system

Buildings & Resources

Seasonal progress revolves around Frost Cores, Ice Blooms, BioLab, and Smart Greenhouse. Your BioLab improves resistance, while your Greenhouse increases Ice Bloom production.

  • Kill Snow Leopards and Frostbanes daily to earn Frost Cores.
  • Use Frost Cores to upgrade Smart Greenhouse.
  • Use Ice Blooms to upgrade BioLab and Greenhouse.
  • Focus BioLab and Greenhouse first before spending heavily on other seasonal buildings.
  • F2P players struggling with higher NPCs can upgrade other buildings for more power.
NPC farming

Frostbane, Snow Leopards, FuryLord, Yetis & Kongs

💥

Frostbane

Main daily target. Higher levels grant better Snowflakes, Frost Cores, and resources.

🐆

Snow Leopards

They use the same attempts as Frostbane but give lower rewards. Use them after Frostbane attempts.

👹

Hunter Yetis

Rally higher level Yetis with main marches. Search for strong levels and avoid already damaged targets.

🦍

Glacier Kongs

Stronger than Yetis and may need multiple rallies. Last hit can matter for kill rewards.

Event Focus

Arctic Ice Pit

Defeat the Tundra Titan to control an Ice Pit and unlock fishing. The alliance that does the most damage claims the pit.

  • Use main formations for Titan fights.
  • Expect injured and perished troops.
  • Use batch healing to reduce speedup and diamond cost.
  • Fishing grants fish index rewards, seasonal resources, badges, fragments, and decoration progress.
Ice Pit Strategy

Fight, Fish, and Coordinate

Ice Pit battles can last a long time and require alliance attendance. You can fish using hooks, share duplicate fish with alliance members, and complete the fish index for rewards.

Weekly events

Frozen Rescue & Snowman Mania

🦣

Frozen Rescue

Runs Monday through Wednesday. Rescue animals and prioritize based on rewards.

🦌

Elk

Elk give badges. With 10 attempts per day, this can be a strong badge source.

⛄

Snowman Mania

Upgrade your Snowman using snowballs. Level 20 grants the Zazz Snowman decoration.

❄️

Core Proliferation

Later event that doubles Frost Core and Ice Bloom gains from NPCs, but rewards only first 15 kills.

Heroes & Mastery

Season Power Choices

Season 2/3 heroes and mastery choices can give major bonuses. Hero swap can help transfer levels and skills, but it does not transfer stars upgraded with fragments.

  • Hero Swap moves hero levels and skill levels, not fragment stars.
  • Star up heroes before swapping to maximize skill book benefits.
  • S2/S3 heroes can provide troop capacity and damage resistance bonuses.
  • Use Support Mastery early, then switch to Military after BioLab level 28.
  • Important mastery picks include Ice Bloom output, Frost Core gain, injury relief, resistance, march speed, and construction speed.
Leadership reminder

Katana Season 2 Rules

  • Follow alliance calls during Ice Pit and server events.
  • Share rescue coordinates and useful NPC targets in Discord.
  • Do not waste high-value attempts on low-value targets when better options are available.
  • Ask leadership before major seasonal decisions if unsure.
